How to read the placement

  • Each star sits at the earliest firm evidence for the religion. Traditions older than any record sit in the undated block on the left.
  • Debated or gradual beginnings appear as a dotted outline on the time band when you select the religion; the gold band shows how long it has lasted, or lasted.
  • Above the band: started by a founding teacher or prophet. Below: grew out of a people's traditions. Borderline cases are explained in each religion's story.
  • Star sizes group followers into four classes, because most counts are estimates. Hollow stars are religions no longer practised.
  • Lines mean grew out of (solid), drew on (dashed) or shares roots with (dotted). They describe historical relationships; they don't judge any faith's own account of its origin.
  • The texts are AI-assisted summaries of general historical knowledge. Key dates and figures were spot-checked, and every link was checked, in October 2026. Dates are approximate and many are debated.
